HEARTSENSE v3.0

Web app + app-style PWA cho quy trinh tim mach da nen tang, san sang de deploy len website.

HEARTSENSE duoc to chuc theo 4 giai doan: onboarding, measurement, analysis/SOS va long-term care. Ban co the test tren browser ngay bay gio, sau do mo rong tiep thanh mobile app va backend production.
